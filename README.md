# ⚡ Namma Electricity — Smart Electricity Intelligence

> **Understand. Predict. Optimize. Save.**
> A context-aware electricity management platform for urban households.

---

##  Overview

**Namma Electricity** helps households understand and optimize electricity usage by combining **consumption data, appliance behaviour, electricity tariffs, subsidy rules, and local outage reliability** into one decision-support platform.

Instead of simply displaying electricity usage, NammaPower turns household data into **personalized, actionable recommendations**.

---

##  Features

### 📊 Consumption Intelligence

* Household and smart-meter consumption analysis
* Peak-usage identification
* Historical consumption trends
* Appliance-level energy estimation

### 💰 Bill & Tariff Analysis

* Electricity bill estimation
* Tariff-aware calculations
* Gruha Jyothi/subsidy analysis
* Consumption threshold awareness
* Potential savings estimation

### 🧠 Smart Recommendations

* Personalized energy-saving recommendations
* Appliance usage optimization
* Peak-load reduction suggestions
* What-if consumption & savings analysis

### 🚨 Local Reliability Intelligence

* Area-wise outage analysis
* Reliability scoring
* Community outage reporting
* Outage-aware energy recommendations

### 🔋 Backup Planning

* Household load estimation
* Inverter/backup capacity recommendations

---

## 🧪 Research Direction

Namma Electricity explores **context-aware residential energy management** by combining:

```text
Consumption + Appliances + Tariffs + Subsidies + Local Reliability
                              ↓
                 Personalized Energy Decisions
```

The research goal is to determine whether combining these contextual factors can produce **more effective household energy recommendations** than consumption-only approaches.
